Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) - Nepal
About CDC

The Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) is the national body responsible for developing and revising the curriculum for school education in Nepal. It operates under the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology.

Key Functions
  • Develop and revise national curriculum for Grade 1-12
  • Prepare curriculum frameworks and guidelines
  • Develop teaching learning materials
  • Conduct research on curriculum issues
  • Provide teacher training on curriculum implementation
